Get GUHSIP on Android

The GUHSIP app opens the same site you use in a browser, full-screen, with no browser bar. Updates reach you automatically.

Download the APK

Android app file, about a few MB. Only download it from this page.

How to install (Android)

1. Tap Download the APK above.

2. When the download finishes, tap the notification, or open your Downloads app and tap guhsip.apk.

3. If your phone says installs from this source are blocked, tap Settings on that screen, switch on Allow from this source, then go back.

4. Tap Install. If Google Play Protect warns you, tap Install anyway. It warns about any app that didn't come from the Play Store.

5. Tap Open, then log in or sign up.

iPhone users

iPhones can't install APK files. Open guhsip.vercel.app in Safari, tap the Share button, then choose Add to Home Screen. You get the same app icon.
